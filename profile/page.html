About me

Hi, I'm Paddy — a radio amateur based just outside Glasgow. I've had a lifelong interest in radio and technology, but it wasn't until early 2025 that I finally sat my UK Foundation licence. I haven't looked back since.

My entry point was actually Meshtastic in 2024 — experimenting with off-grid LoRa mesh networking got me hooked on radio in general. From there I got licensed to get on DMR, then discovered the joy of FM simplex contacts, and more recently dove into HF. Each mode has pulled me deeper down the rabbit hole.

Meshtastic & MeshCore

I've been active in the central belt Scotland Meshtastic community since 2024 and it's grown enormously. We hit a coast-to-coast coverage milestone in late 2025 and the network continues to expand northward. My nodes are named with the PDY prefix — PDY4 is my usual daily node on LongFast. Say hi if you hear me.

I also maintain MeshFlow, a Meshtastic monitoring tool. More recently I've been active on MeshCore as an alternative encrypted mesh platform alongside Meshtastic.

DMR

DMR was what got me licensed in the first place. I almost always have a DMR handheld on me, and with GB7GL covering most of the west of Scotland I'm regularly listening to the Scottish talkgroups. The TYT MD-9600 lives in the car for mobile use.

HF

I picked up the IC-7300 after outgrowing the G90 — what a rig. My antenna situation is a work in progress: a 22m long wire across the garden into a 9:1 unun gives me receive across 80m–10m and reasonable transmit from 40m–10m. A 6m dipole and a 20m Moxon are on the build list once I solve the mounting puzzle.

Building & electronics

I enjoy building things — both electronics and physical fabrication. My proudest project to date is a custom portable DMR hotspot: off-the-shelf internals in a tidy enclosure with battery power and USB charging. It's that rare build that just works.

I've also built a lot of Meshtastic nodes — solar-powered WisBlocks, 3D-printed Heltec V3 cases, and tiny Seeed Xiao nodes hidden inside scavenged vape pens. For daily use though I mostly reach for the Seeed Tracker.
